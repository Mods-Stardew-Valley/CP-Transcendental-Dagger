# Descritpion

    For years, Marlon has held onto a collection of dusty blueprints entrusted to him by your grandfather. Now that you’ve proven your worth in the Adventurer's Guild, it’s time to fulfill a long-lost dream. This mod introduces a unique 10-tier evolution system for a custom dagger that grows alongside your skill and courage.
